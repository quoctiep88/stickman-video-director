## A — approved excerpt

You tap a weather app. A moment later, tomorrow's forecast appears. How did your phone get that information?

It sent an API request. An API is a set of rules that lets one piece of software ask another for information or an action.

Think of a clearly labeled service counter. Your app knows where to send the request, what details to include, and how the answer will be organized.

## B — proposed excerpt

You tap your weather app, and a moment later there's tomorrow's forecast. Where did it come from?

Your app asked for it. That was an API request. An API gives one program the rules for asking another for information or for asking it to do something.

Think of a labeled service counter. You know where to go and what details to give. Your app also knows what form the answer will take.
