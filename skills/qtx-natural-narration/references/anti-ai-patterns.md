# Pattern audit

Look for habits in context. Keep any phrase that earns its place.

- Test generic openings such as “Have you ever wondered…”, “In today's world…”, “Let's dive into…”, “At its core…” and “Imagine a world where…”. Try starting at the first observable event.
- Circle repeated “This means…”, “In other words…” and “So remember…”. If the next sentence merely repeats the last one, cut it. Check for recaps after every beat and overly perfect conclusions.
- Listen for uniform sentence length, three-part lists everywhere, overly balanced parallel clauses and repeated “It's not X. It's Y.” contrasts. Give each fact the space it needs.
- Check excessive transitions, rhetorical questions, generic enthusiasm, unnecessary adjectives and clusters of abstract nouns. Name the actor and the action when that makes the idea clearer.

Judge by ear and meaning. Detector scores, deliberate errors, imitation of a named living creator and filler added for casual effect have no role in the review.
